Play RX
=======

Play RX receives analog 5.8 GHz FPV video with RTL8812BU USB Wi-Fi adapters used as
software-defined radios, and serves each feed's video to the app window, to OBS, to FPV
Trackside and as Windows cameras.

Built with
----------
- kepyur (analog FPV video receiver for RTL8812BU adapters) - GPL-2.0
- wifi-sdr (raw sample streaming from the adapter) - GPL-2.0-only
- devourer (userspace Realtek USB driver) - GPL-2.0 (licenses\GPL-2.0.txt)
- libusb - LGPL-2.1 (licenses\LGPL-2.1-libusb.txt)
- winpthreads (MinGW-w64 runtime) - MIT-style (licenses\winpthreads.txt)
- libwdi (driver setup, driver\wdi-simple.exe) - LGPL-3.0 (licenses\LGPL-3.0-libwdi.txt); it carries
  Microsoft's WinUSB / WDF co-installers from the Windows Driver Kit 8 redistributable components
- webview (window) - MIT; uses the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, part of Windows

Firmware
--------
devourer includes Realtek's firmware for the RTL8822B chip (hal/hal8822b_fw.c), which is
built into PlayRX.exe and loaded onto the adapter. It belongs to Realtek, is not covered by
the GPL, and is distributed as devourer distributes it.

Your data
---------
Settings, feeds and adapter names are kept in %APPDATA%\Play RX; recordings in
Videos\Play RX unless another folder is chosen. Uninstalling leaves them in place.
