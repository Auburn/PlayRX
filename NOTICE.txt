Play RX
=======

Play RX receives analog 5.8 GHz FPV video with RTL8812BU USB Wi-Fi adapters used as
software-defined radios, and serves each feed's video to the app window, to OBS, to FPV
Trackside and (on Windows) as cameras for other programs. It shows live video only and
does not record.

- libusb - LGPL-2.1 (licenses\LGPL-2.1-libusb.txt)

Firmware
--------
devourer includes Realtek's firmware for the RTL8822B chip (hal/hal8822b_fw.c), which is
built into the Play RX program and loaded onto the adapter. It belongs to Realtek, is not covered by
the GPL, and is distributed as devourer distributes it.

Your data
---------
Settings, feeds and adapter names are kept in %APPDATA%\Play RX (Windows),
~/Library/Application Support/Play RX (macOS) or ~/.local/share/play-rx (Linux).
Uninstalling leaves them in place.
